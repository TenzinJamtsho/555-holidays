Our story

We started 555 Holidays with a simple wish: to show visitors the Bhutan we grew up in.

For more than 20 years we've planned private journeys built around monasteries, festivals and Himalayan scenery, with local guides we call Chief Experience Officers, who live in the valleys they show you.

We believe in tourism the Bhutanese way: high value, low impact. That means working with local communities, protecting nature and culture, and keeping the balance of adventure, culture and rest that Gross National Happiness asks of all of us.
