About 555 Holidays

We grew up in Bhutan. For us, it is a gift from our kings: a land still untouched, much like the world looked before modernism.

There are hundreds of travel agents here, and most trips look the same: monasteries, mountain roads, long hours in a car. That history is the same everywhere, and you can read it in a book.

But for us, Bhutan is something else. It is waking up slowly. Breakfast with no rush. No traffic, no tight schedule, and time to take our own time.

That is why we started 555 Holidays: to show you Bhutan as we know it, not as everyone else shows it.
