03Who we are

Why travel with us

We're a small, family-run agency from Thimphu. Bhutan isn't our product; it's our home.

Nothing on our trips is staged. The farmhouse lunch is with a family we've known for years; at a festival you'll stand among neighbours. Most trips are just you and your companions, so we can change plans whenever a valley asks you to stay a little longer.

We hire locally, choose family-run lodges, and return part of every booking to the communities and forests that make Bhutan what it is.
