Why travel with us

Why travel with us

We're a small, family-run agency from Thimphu. Bhutan isn't our product; it's our home.

Nothing on our trips is staged for visitors. When you sit down to lunch in a farmhouse, it's a family we've known for years. When you watch a festival, you're among neighbours. We keep groups small (most trips are just you and your companions), so we can change plans when a valley invites you to linger.

And we try to leave every place a little better: we hire locally, choose family-run lodges, and put part of every booking back into the communities and forests that make Bhutan what it is.
